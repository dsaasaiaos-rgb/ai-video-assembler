# AI Video Assembler - App Guide

## Overview

**AI Video Assembler** is a mobile app that helps you create professional 1-minute videos by combining AI-generated content. Write a script, let the app break it into scenes, generate images and voiceovers, and assemble everything into a finished video.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating Your First Project

1. **Open the app** and tap **"New Project"** on the Home screen
2. **Enter a project name** (e.g., "Product Launch", "Tutorial Video")
3. **Choose an aspect ratio**:
   - **9:16** for vertical videos (mobile, stories)
   - **16:9** for horizontal videos (YouTube, presentations)
4. **Tap "Create"** to start your project

---

## Workflow: Script → Scenes → Content → Timeline

### Step 1: Write Your Script

1. Open your project and go to the **Script** tab
2. Tap **"Write Script"** to open the script editor
3. **Write your video script** describing:
   - What happens in each scene
   - Who speaks and what they say
   - Key visuals and transitions
   - Any important details

**Example:**
```
Scene 1: A woman walks into a modern coffee shop, smiling.
She orders a cappuccino from the barista.

Scene 2: Close-up of the espresso machine brewing.
Voiceover: "Our coffee is made with freshly roasted beans."

Scene 3: The woman takes a sip and looks satisfied.
She gives a thumbs up to the camera.
```

4. **Tap "Save Script"** when done
5. The app will automatically break your script into scenes

### Step 2: Plan Your Scenes

1. Go to the **Scenes** tab to see your video broken into individual clips
2. Each scene shows:
   - Scene number and description
   - Allocated duration
3. **Tap any scene** to generate content for it

### Step 3: Generate Content

1. **Tap a scene** from the Scenes tab
2. The **Content Generator** opens with options:

#### Generate Image
- The AI will create a visual based on your scene description
- **Tap "Generate Image"** to create an AI image
- **Tap "Regenerate"** if you want a different version
- The image will be used as the visual for this scene

#### Generate Voiceover
- **Enter the voiceover text** (narration or dialogue)
- **Tap "Generate Voiceover"** to create AI audio
- The voiceover will play during this scene
- **Tap "Regenerate Voiceover"** to try a different voice or tone

3. **Tap "Done"** when satisfied with the content

### Step 4: Assemble Your Timeline

1. Go to the **Timeline** tab to see all your clips arranged
2. The timeline shows:
   - **Total duration** vs. your 1-minute target
   - **Progress bar** showing how much time you've used
   - **All clips** with their content status (image ✓, voiceover ✓)

#### Timeline Controls
- **Tap a clip** to edit or regenerate its content
- **Tap "Edit"** to adjust the scene
- **Tap "Delete"** to remove a scene
- **"Preview"** button to watch your video
- **"Export"** button to save your finished video

#### Timing Tips
- **Green bar** = Perfect timing (60 seconds)
- **Yellow bar** = Room to add more content
- **Red bar** = Video is too long, trim some scenes

### Step 5: Export Your Video

1. When your timeline is complete, **tap "Export"**
2. Choose where to save:
   - Camera roll (gallery)
   - Share directly to social media
   - Download to files
3. **Wait for processing** (usually 30-60 seconds)
4. Your finished 1-minute video is ready!

## Tips for Great Videos

### Script Writing
- **Be specific**: Describe visuals, emotions, and actions clearly
- **Keep it concise**: 1 minute = ~150 words of narration
- **Use transitions**: Describe how scenes connect (fade, cut, zoom)
- **Include timing hints**: "Quick scene (3 seconds)" or "Slow pan (8 seconds)"

### Content Generation
- **Detailed descriptions** = Better AI images
- **Natural voiceover text** = Better audio quality
- **Regenerate if needed**: Try different prompts or tones
- **Mix content types**: Combine images, text overlays, and voiceovers

### Timeline Assembly
- **Watch the progress bar**: Stay within your 1-minute target
- **Balance pacing**: Mix fast and slow scenes
- **Preview often**: Check your video before exporting
- **Adjust durations**: Make scenes longer or shorter as needed

2. **Tap the trash icon** on any project

## Troubleshooting

### Content Generation Fails
- **Check your internet connection**
- **Try regenerating** the content
- **Simplify your description** if it's very long
- **Try different wording** for better results

### Video Export Takes Too Long
- **Check your internet**: Export requires uploading
- **Reduce video quality** if available
- **Try exporting again** if it times out

### App Crashes or Freezes
- **Close and reopen** the app
- **Check available storage** on your device
- **Update the app** to the latest version
- **Restart your device** if problems persist

## FAQ

**Q: How long does it take to create a video?**
A: Usually 5-10 minutes depending on how many scenes you have and how many times you regenerate content.

**Q: Can I edit my script after creating scenes?**
A: Yes! Go to the Script tab and tap "Edit Script" anytime. Your scenes will update automatically.

**Q: What if I don't like the generated image?**
A: Tap the scene, then tap "Regenerate Image" to create a different version.

**Q: Can I use my own images instead of AI-generated ones?**
A: Not yet, but this feature is coming soon!

**Q: How many projects can I create?**
A: Unlimited! Create as many projects as you want.

**Q: Can I share my video before exporting?**
A: Export first, then share from your camera roll or files app.
